A bot ends its reply with a fenced `bothub` block. `outputs.ts` reads and validates it, and
`apply.ts` carries out what was accepted. Concepts are on [Proposals](/docs/proposals).

## Applying

<Sym name="apply" /> pushes first, then applies the rest in order. Each effect has a stable key, is
journalled as `pending` in the run's outcome before anything happens, and is recorded again as it
lands. A retry after a crash finds what an earlier run already did and does not repeat it.

Replies, labels and questions go through the [issues interface](/docs/reference/issues). Pushes go
through the run's worktree (see <Sym name="workspaces" />), and the pipeline calls `apply` from
`settle` once the job's checks pass (see [the until loop](/docs/reference/pipeline#the-until-loop)).
