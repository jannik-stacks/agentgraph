## The until loop

A bot job runs, then each check in its `until` runs against the worktree. Findings from failing
gating checks go back to the same bot as a follow-up prompt, until the checks pass, `until.max`
follow-ups are used or the bot's `usd-per-run` is spent. The loop is in

<Sym name="attempts" file="src/pipeline/attempt.ts" />. Every check ends in a verdict.

<Fields name="Verdict" />

A check gates when its mode is not `log`. A skipped check does not gate, unless no gating check
ran at all. Once the follow-ups are spent, only failing `block` checks fail the run; `warn` ones are
recorded and let it through. A `blocked` verdict means the check could not run, and the job asks a
human instead of sending the bot after it.

Two jobs never work on one branch at the same time. A job whose branch another live run holds
waits for it, up to its own timeout, and is deferred only after that.

## Verdicts

A check is a script (exit code), a bot that replies `PASS` or `FAIL`, or a `decide` check that asks
its body as a yes or no question to a small local model (<Sym name="decideCheck" />). A bot that
does not answer clearly fails. A check that fans out over files, hunks or script items passes only
when every item passes, and its findings are labelled by item.

When a job's final run ends, <Sym name="summarize" /> writes a short memory of it next to its
events, without a model.
